Privacy & cookies.

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Who operates this website

The Free Website Co is an initiative by Gotter, a trading name of Mullens Ltd, registered in England and Wales under company number 17109449. Our registered office is 46 Sparsholt Road, Southampton, England, SO19 9NJ.

Browsing this website

Vercel hosts this website. Its infrastructure may process technical information including your IP address, requested pages, device information and access time to deliver and protect the site and investigate errors. This supports our legitimate interest in running a reliable website.

The enquiry tool

The enquiry tool works locally in your browser. Names, business details, email addresses and project descriptions entered into it are not submitted to us or saved in browser storage by this website. Copying a brief puts it on your device’s clipboard. Saving a brief downloads a text file to your device. Avoid including sensitive personal information.

Sending an email

Where a receiving email address is displayed, choosing Email enquiry opens a draft in your email application. You decide whether to send it. Your email provider then processes the message under its own terms. If no receiving email is displayed, the website can only prepare, copy or save the brief; it cannot send us an enquiry.

Correspondence

If you contact us, we use the information you provide to respond, understand your requirements and discuss a possible project. The basis is taking steps at your request before a contract, where applicable, or our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries. An enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing. We keep correspondence only while needed to deal with it, any resulting work and applicable record-keeping obligations. Relevant email and hosting providers may process information on our behalf; providers may process information outside the UK under their applicable transfer safeguards.

Cookies and advertising

This website does not add analytics, advertising pixels, non-essential cookies or local-storage tracking. No advertising consent banner is used because these features are not installed. We will update this notice and add appropriate choices before introducing non-essential tracking. Our hosting provider operates technical infrastructure needed to serve and protect the site.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict processing of your personal data, object to processing or request portability. We do not use the enquiry tool for automated decisions. For a privacy request, use the enquiry email if displayed, or write to our registered office above. You can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Changes

We will update this notice when the website’s handling of information changes, including before enabling server-delivered enquiries, analytics or advertising tracking.
