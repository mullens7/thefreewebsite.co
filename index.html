Frequently asked questions

LET’S CLEAR A FEW THINGS UP.

Why is the website development free?

Gotter created this initiative to make professional websites more accessible to small businesses and startups. We don’t charge an upfront development fee for the agreed website build. You pay an ongoing monthly fee for hosting, maintenance, technical support and your domain under the agreed arrangements.

Who will build my website?

Gotter designs, develops and maintains your website. The Free Website Co is an initiative by Gotter, rather than a separate development agency.

What will I pay each month?

We confirm your monthly fee once we understand what you need, before work begins. Hosting, maintenance, technical support and your domain are included under the arrangements we agree.

Can you build an online shop?

Tell us about the products you want to sell and the features you need. We’ll agree the scope first. Websites taking payments carry a small transaction charge to cover third-party payment processing costs, with the rate explained upfront.

I already have a domain. Can I use it?

Yes, tell us which domain you already have. We’ll discuss the connection, registration and ongoing arrangements with you before starting.

What about extra features and changes?

Features or services outside the agreed scope may incur additional charges. We’ll explain and agree those costs before undertaking the extra work.

How long does it take?

The timeline depends on your project and the content you have ready. We’ll agree a realistic schedule before work begins.

Are there contracts or cancellation terms?

The project agreement explains the monthly plan, responsibilities, domain arrangements and cancellation terms before you commit. Enquiring does not create a service contract.
