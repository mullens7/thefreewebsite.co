A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Fair questions.
Straight answers.

Who’s behind The Free Website Co?＋

The Free Website Co is an initiative by Gotter, the website and software development brand that develops and maintains your website. It’s Gotter’s accessible website programme for small businesses and startups.

Is the website really free to build?＋

Yes. Gotter designs and develops your website without an upfront development fee, within the scope we agree together. An ongoing monthly fee covers hosting, maintenance, technical support and your domain under the agreed arrangements. If your project takes payments, a small transaction charge covers third-party processing costs.

What kinds of businesses can you help?＋

We’re here for small businesses getting started or taking their next step online. Whether you offer a service or sell products, tell us what you have in mind and we’ll discuss whether the initiative is right for you. For broader software or digital projects, Gotter can discuss a separate scope and quote.

Will it be a custom website?＋

Yes. Gotter designs and builds around your business, branding and agreed requirements. The starting point is a conversation about you and your customers.

How much is the monthly fee?＋

We’ll confirm the monthly fee once we understand your project, before any work begins. It covers hosting, maintenance, technical support and your domain under the agreed arrangements. Payment processing charges and any extras are explained separately.

How long does a build take?＋

It depends on the scope and the content you have ready. We’ll agree a realistic timeline with you before starting.

What do I need to get started?＋

Just a little information about your business and what you’d like to achieve. If you have a logo, photos or existing domain, great. If you’re still figuring things out, that’s fine too.
