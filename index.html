FAQs

Good questions, straight answers.

Something we haven’t covered? Email hello@gotter.co.uk or call +44 7796 184 248.

Why is the website development free?

Gotter created this initiative to make professional websites more accessible to small businesses and startups. We don’t charge an upfront development fee for the agreed website build. You pay an ongoing monthly fee for hosting, maintenance, technical support and your domain under the agreed arrangements.

Who will build my website?

Gotter designs, develops and maintains your website. The Free Website Co. is an initiative by Gotter, rather than a separate agency or a self-service website builder.

What will I pay each month?

We confirm your monthly fee once we understand what you need, before work begins. Hosting, maintenance, technical support and your domain are included under the arrangements we agree.

Can you build an online shop?

Tell us about the products you want to sell and the features you need, and we’ll agree the scope first. Websites that take payments carry a small transaction charge to cover third-party payment processing costs, with the rate explained upfront. Larger or more complex shops may suit Gotter’s bespoke services instead.

I already have a domain. Can I use it?

Yes. Tell us which domain you have, and we’ll discuss connecting it, registration and the ongoing arrangements with you before starting.

What about extra features and changes?

Features or services outside the agreed scope may incur additional charges. We’ll explain and agree those costs before doing the extra work.

How long does it take?

It depends on your project and the content you have ready. We’ll agree a realistic schedule before work begins.

Are there contracts or cancellation terms?

Your project agreement sets out the monthly plan, responsibilities, domain arrangements and cancellation terms before you commit. Getting in touch does not create a contract.

Need something bigger?

Gotter builds bespoke, too.

The Free Website Co. is designed for getting a business online. For a larger website, a more complex online shop or custom software, Gotter’s bespoke services are priced as a project, with scope, price and timing agreed in a written proposal.
