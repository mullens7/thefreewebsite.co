A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Fair questions.
Straight answers.

Is the website really free to build?＋

Yes. We don’t charge a design or development fee for the website or software we agree to build. There is a monthly fee covering hosting, maintenance and your domain. If your project takes payments, a small transaction charge covers third-party processing costs.

What kinds of businesses can you help?＋

We’re here for small businesses getting started or taking their next step online. Whether you offer a service, sell products or have a software idea, tell us what you have in mind and we’ll discuss whether we can help.

Will it be a custom website?＋

Yes. We design and build around your business, branding and agreed requirements. The starting point is a conversation about you and your customers.

How much is the monthly fee?＋

We’ll confirm the monthly fee once we understand your project, before any work begins. It covers hosting, maintenance and your domain. Payment processing charges are explained separately.

How long does a build take?＋

It depends on the scope and the content you have ready. We’ll agree a realistic timeline with you before starting.

What do I need to get started?＋

Just a little information about your business and what you’d like to achieve. If you have a logo, photos or existing domain, great. If you’re still figuring things out, that’s fine too.
