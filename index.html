04 / FAIR QUESTIONS

“Hang on.
Really?”

Yes, really. Here’s the detail.

So, what’s actually free?＋

The upfront design and development of the website we agree to build. An ongoing monthly fee covers hosting, maintenance, technical support and your domain under the agreed arrangements. Payment processing charges apply where relevant.

Who’s doing the building?＋

Gotter, the website and software development brand behind this initiative. Gotter designs, develops and maintains your website.

Is this right for my business?＋

It’s for small businesses, startups and new ventures that need a professional presence online. Tell us what you need and we’ll discuss whether the initiative fits. Broader software projects can be scoped separately with Gotter.

How much is the monthly fee?＋

We confirm the monthly cost once we understand your website, before work begins. Hosting, maintenance, technical support and your domain are included under the agreed arrangements. Any extras and processing charges are explained separately.

What about changes or cancellation?＋

We’ll agree the plan, responsibilities, domain arrangements and cancellation terms in writing before you commit. Features beyond the agreed project scope may be quoted separately.

What do you need from me?＋

An idea of what your business does and what your website should achieve. A logo, photos or existing domain are useful if you have them. We’ll agree the content, scope and timeline together.
